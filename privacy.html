Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

The short version: ValPulse doesn't collect, sell or share any of your data. There are no ValPulse accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app keeps stays on your device.

Signing in

You sign in on Riot Games' official sign-in page, shown inside the app. Your username and password go directly to Riot Games and are never seen, stored or sent anywhere by ValPulse. After you sign in, the app keeps Riot's session cookie on your device so you stay signed in. Signing out of an account in the app deletes its session.

What stays on your device

  • Riot's sign-in session for each account you add
  • Your settings (theme, language, accent colour)
  • Your wishlist, loadout presets and shop history
  • Temporary copies of game art, to load faster

This information never leaves your device except as described below. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Which servers the app talks to

ServerWhy
Riot Games (auth.riotgames.com, *.a.pvp.net and related)To sign you in and load your own account data: shop, wallet, battle pass, missions, matches, collection and loadout, and to save loadout changes you make.
valorant-api.comPublic game art and names (skins, agents, maps). No personal data is sent.
esports-api.service.valorantesports.comThe public pro esports schedule and results. No personal data is sent.

Your use of Riot Games' services is covered by Riot Games' Privacy Notice.

What we don't do

  • No ValPulse servers: the developer never receives your data.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Children

ValPulse doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
